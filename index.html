🇺🇸 Official Executive Decree (English)
Mr. HUO and the Executive Board of HUOKAING THARA BANK are pleased to officially announce the upcoming grand launch of HUOKAING THARA BANK and HUOKAING THARA ISP in the near future!
👔📈 Mr. LEN is formally appointed as Chief Executive Officer (CEO), replacing Mr. MEN, leading overall corporate strategy, organizational vision, and general business development.
💼 Mrs. KIMMUY is formally appointed as Secretary and Chief of Customer Service. She plays a vital role in managing executive administrative workflows, coordinating high-level corporate documentation, and ensuring that all client touchpoints maintain world-class standards of responsiveness, care, and professional excellence.
👔📈 Mr. DOM is formally appointed as Chief Executive Officer (CEO), steering core digital banking growth, strategic enterprise vision, and overall business operations.
👔📈 Mr. NAK is formally appointed as Chief Executive Officer (CEO), replacing Mr. DO, driving ISP expansion, telecommunications infrastructure, and high-speed connectivity scale across regions.
👔📈 Mr. KUO is formally appointed as Chief Executive Officer (CEO), managing financial asset allocation, strategic enterprise partnerships, and technical innovation initiatives.
👔 Mr. SENG CHHAT is formally appointed as Director, overseeing corporate governance, direction, and total operational execution, as well as confirming these executive transitions.
🛡️🔐 Mr. JAV is formally appointed as Chief of Security, supervising physical security operations, facility safety, and core protection frameworks.
🛡️📈 Mr. HUOKAING THARA is formally appointed as Chief Cybersecurity Officer & Head of Marketing, leading network defense, transaction security, and strategic market expansion.
🕯️ Approval & Transition Notice:
This executive decision was approved by Mr. Huo and Mr. B, and confirmed by Mr. Seng Chhat (Director).

📌 Notice: Due to the deeply tragic passing of former executives Mr. DO and Mr. MEN, these changes have been enacted. May both of them rest in peace.

HUOKAING THARA BANK & ISP are committed to delivering secure, high-speed connectivity and advanced digital financial solutions.
